Retail Sales · Markets · Tuesday Edition

Retail Sales Due as Bitcoin Stabilizes and Markets Drift Lower Ahead of the Report

The Census Bureau releases its October retail sales report at 8:30 a.m. ET today, with economists broadly expecting a modest gain following September's surprisingly strong 0.8% increase. This edition goes to press roughly ninety minutes before the number itself is known. The report arrives as the market's last major scheduled data point before Wednesday's release of FOMC minutes from the Fed's late-October meeting, the first detailed look inside Chair Kevin Warsh's deliberations since recent hawkish commentary from several regional Fed presidents has revived talk of a possible rate hike rather than a cut at December's meeting.

Bitcoin showed its first signs of stabilizing Tuesday, edging up to roughly $64,850 after two consecutive days of steep losses — a tentative sign that the leveraged unwind that began over the weekend may be running its course. Equities drifted lower ahead of the retail sales report, with the S&P 500 down 0.3% to 7,669.19 and the Nasdaq leading losses, as the AI-stock selloff that has gripped markets since Nvidia's abrupt leadership change earlier this month continues to weigh on sentiment.

Brent crude held steady near $118 through a tenth straight quiet week in the Gulf. The Bulls topped Minnesota 117-106 Saturday while the Blackhawks blew a 4-0 lead in a shootout loss to Winnipeg, and Chicago continued its mild stretch with a high near 50°F.

Health, Wellness & Fitness

Research: New Molecular Therapy Shows Promise Against Treatment-Resistant Prostate Cancer in Lab Studies

Researchers at the University of Michigan's Rogel Cancer Center have developed a new class of molecules, nicknamed "DALTACs," that act as a kind of molecular brace, locking the androgen receptor and a helper protein called p300 together in an abnormal, inactive configuration. Published in the journal Nature, the lead compound proved roughly a thousand times more potent against tumor cells than combining separate androgen-receptor and p300 inhibitors, according to the study's senior author. The approach was tested in cell lines, mouse models, and tumor-like structures grown from patient tissue, producing durable responses even against cancers resistant to standard treatment. The work remains preclinical, with no human trials yet underway, and the university notes that two of the researchers hold equity in a company that has licensed the compounds.

Random Fact

The Census Bureau's Advance Monthly Retail Trade report is based on a sample of roughly 4,700 employer firms, yet it's treated as one of the most market-moving economic releases precisely because consumer spending drives close to 70% of U.S. GDP.

Word of the Day
brace

(verb/noun, from Old French brace "the two arms") — something that holds two parts rigidly together. Today's featured cancer research uses the term literally: its lead molecule physically locks two proteins into a fixed, inactive position rather than blocking either one individually.
