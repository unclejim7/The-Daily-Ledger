Bitcoin Extends Its Slide as the AI-Stock Selloff Carries Into the New Week

Bitcoin's weekend plunge deepened into Monday, with BTC slipping further to roughly $64,300 — a second straight day of steep losses following Sunday's sudden overnight selloff. Health, Wellness & Fitness

Study: Low Testosterone Linked to 18% Higher Risk of Cancer Death in Large Pooled Analysis

The Androgens In Men Study (AIMS), led by researchers at the University of Western Australia and published in The Lancet Healthy Longevity, pooled data from more than 26,000 men across eleven cohorts in Australia, the United States, and Europe. The analysis found that men with very low testosterone had an 18% higher risk of dying from cancer later in life. Notably, the pattern did not hold for prostate cancer specifically — researchers found no link between low testosterone and prostate-cancer mortality. The study's authors caution that the findings show an association rather than proven causation, and explicitly say the results do not support taking testosterone supplements as a way to reduce cancer risk.
