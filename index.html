Mortgage Rate Ticks Down to 7.28% as Freddie Mac's New Reading Arrives; Bitcoin's Record Streak Finally Snaps at Eleven Days

Thursday's new Freddie Mac reading nudged the 30-year mortgage rate down to 7.28%, a modest but welcome third straight easing after a stretch of sideways movement earlier in the week. The decline tracks a broader trend that has seen the rate drift gradually lower over the past month, offering some incremental relief to buyers who have spent most of 2026 contending with rates well above 7%.

This publication flagged Wednesday that the market has rarely rewarded a run that clean with a twelfth day without at least a pause, and today's modest pullback is exactly that: a rest, not a reversal, with price still comfortably above the round-number level it took eleven sessions to clear.

Health, Wellness & Fitness

Study: Full-Fat Dairy Did Not Worsen Weight or Cholesterol in 12-Week Trial, Challenging Old Low-Fat Guidance

A University of Toronto trial published in the Journal of Nutrition followed 74 adults with overweight or obesity across three diet groups for twelve weeks: a calorie-restricted low-dairy plan, an energy-neutral diet including three daily servings of full-fat dairy, and an unrestricted diet also including three daily dairy servings. Contrary to decades of low-fat dietary advice, full-fat dairy did not worsen weight, LDL cholesterol, or insulin resistance in either dairy group, and those groups also saw improved blood pressure along with higher calcium, protein, and vitamin D intake. Researchers point to the "dairy matrix" hypothesis — the idea that nutrients within a food interact with one another rather than acting in isolation — to explain the result. The study's authors caution that the sample size was small and the trial short, so the findings don't establish that full-fat dairy is better than low-fat, only that it wasn't measurably worse on these markers.
