Bears Rout Saints Behind a Dominant Defense, Improve to 8-2 on a Five-Game Winning Streak

The Bears throttled New Orleans 21-13 at Soldier Field Sunday, extending their winning streak to five games and improving to 8-2. Caleb Williams threw for 181 yards and two touchdowns, finding D'Andre Swift and Colston Loveland both in the second quarter as Chicago built a 16-0 halftime lead. But the story was the defense: six total sacks, with Jeffery Simmons accounting for three of them, and a fourth-quarter safety off a Montez Sweat pressure sealed the win. The Bears now turn to a Thanksgiving Day showdown at Detroit.

Markets rallied to open Thanksgiving week, with the S&P 500 gaining 1.0% to 7,707.11 as relief from last week's selloff and continued momentum around recent tech-sector listings lifted sentiment broadly. Bitcoin extended its stabilization, edging up to roughly $61,050 for a second consecutive gain after last week's sharp six-day decline.

Brent crude held steady near $118 through a sixteenth straight quiet week in the Gulf. The Blackhawks won in overtime at Calgary for their first road win of the season, but the result was overshadowed by news that Connor Bedard will need shoulder surgery and faces a lengthy absence. Chicago enjoyed a sunny, mild start to the holiday week, with a high near 45°F.

Health, Wellness & Fitness

Study: Pilot Trial Finds Fiber Blend Eases IBS Symptoms Without Worsening Bloating or Pain

Researchers at Edith Cowan University in Australia tested a blended fiber supplement combining resistant maize starch and psyllium husk powder in a small, randomized, double-blind pilot trial of adults with irritable bowel syndrome following a low-FODMAP diet. Published in the European Journal of Nutrition, the three-week study of roughly two dozen participants found the supplement appeared to improve gut health markers without worsening common IBS symptoms such as pain, bloating, diarrhea, or constipation. A separate, larger systematic review published this fall in Gastroenterology has also supported fiber supplementation, particularly psyllium, as a reasonable first-line therapy for IBS. Researchers caution that the pilot trial's small size means its specific findings need to be confirmed in larger studies before drawing firm conclusions.

Random Fact

An NFL safety, worth two points, is the rarest scoring play in the sport — teams average fewer than one safety per sixteen games league-wide in a typical season, making Sunday's fourth-quarter safety a genuinely uncommon sight.

Word of the Day
resistant starch

(noun, functional classification coined in the 1980s) — a type of starch that resists digestion in the small intestine and instead ferments in the colon, functioning more like dietary fiber than a typical carbohydrate. Today's featured IBS study used a resistant starch derived from maize as part of its fiber blend.
