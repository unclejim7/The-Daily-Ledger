Markets · Chicago Sports · Monday Edition

Markets Hold Steady Ahead of Tomorrow's Inflation Test as the Bears Roll to a Seventh Win

Markets opened the week in a holding pattern ahead of tomorrow's October CPI report, widely seen as the single most consequential data point before the Fed's December meeting. The S&P 500 rose 0.1% to 7,678.64, the Dow added a similar amount, and the Nasdaq gained 0.2% — modest moves consistent with a market waiting on real information rather than making a directional bet of its own.

Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee has already flagged part of what's at stake, calling recent services inflation readings "a little distressingly high" in remarks that will be tested directly by tomorrow's number. Brent crude held near $118 a barrel through a third consecutive quiet night in the Gulf, now the longest calm stretch since the Saudi counterstrikes began.

Bitcoin climbed to roughly $81,000, a ninth straight day of gains, and the 30-year mortgage holds at 7.31% ahead of Thursday's new reading. The Bears provided Monday's best news with a decisive 27-20 win over Tampa Bay Sunday night, improving to 7-2 on a national stage.

Health, Wellness & Fitness

Study: Daily Peanut Consumption Linked to Improved Brain Insulin Response and Memory in Older Adults

A study published in Food & Function found that healthy adults aged 60 to 75 who ate roughly two servings of peanuts daily for 16 weeks showed beneficial changes in insulin responsiveness in brain regions associated with memory. A related companion study found a 5.8% increase in verbal memory performance among participants, though other cognitive measures showed no significant change. Researchers caution the trial was small and did not use an energy- or nutrient-matched control food, and say further research is needed to confirm whether the observed changes in brain insulin response translate into meaningful real-world cognitive benefits.
